Our Story

Born in Flint.
Built for the Nation.

Ascend Institute was born from a simple but powerful idea—that every person, regardless of circumstance, deserves the opportunity to build a strong foundation, pursue meaningful growth, and reach their full potential.

Ascend's roots trace back to Be Ready Enrichment Services, a nationally award-winning workforce enrichment program that transformed hundreds of lives inside NorthGate Supply Chain Services Company in Flint, Michigan. Be Ready was grounded in NorthGate's mission to bless people and inspired by founder Jack Goggins' belief that people should always be ready for new opportunities.

Designed around Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs, the Be Ready model was built on a commitment to meeting people where they are with dignity and respect, while empowering them to move forward with confidence.

What began as a commitment to employees evolved into a proven model for transformation. Through innovative programming, strategic partnerships, and a practical framework for growth and development, Be Ready earned local, national, and international recognition for its innovative approach to workforce enrichment, its commitment to professional development, and the meaningful impact it had on the lives of those it served.

Today, Ascend Institute carries that proven approach beyond NorthGate's walls and into the wider community. What started as one man's commitment to bless people grew into a distinguished model for personal and professional development. Now, Ascend Institute continues that legacy—blessing people through transformative training and development for all who are ready to ascend.

The Ascend Pathway

Real People.
Real Transformation.

Grounded in Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

Our progressive model meets people exactly where they are — then walks with them all the way to where they're called to be. This is what Ascend looks like on the ground.

Phase One

Meeting immediate needs. Restoring stability.

Before someone can grow, they need to be stable. Ascend's Ignite phase removes the barriers that keep people from showing up — transportation, housing, emergency needs, and resource navigation.

Grounded in Safety & Physiological Needs

Phase Two

Building skills. Strengthening confidence.

Financial literacy, legal assistance, felony expungement, and Getting Ahead at Work training — practical tools that turn a temporary job into a sustainable future. Shift is where barriers become stepping stones.

Grounded in Love, Belonging & Esteem

Phase Three

Developing leaders. Creating opportunity.

Eight weeks. Cohort-based. Transformative. Launch turns high-potential individuals into leaders equipped to elevate their families, teams, and communities through emotional intelligence and vision.

Grounded in Self-Actualization
